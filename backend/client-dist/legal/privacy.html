8. FRAUD REPORTING AND COMPLAINT HANDLING

ONLOK may process complaint submissions, fraud-related reports, and

dispute records for purposes including:

  • complaint review and investigation
  • fraud detection and prevention
  • operational integrity protection
  • dispute resolution
  • evidentiary preservation
  • regulatory compliance
  • lawful disclosure obligations

Users submitting complaints acknowledge that records may be reviewed

internally for operational, investigative, compliance, or evidentiary

purposes.

Where a complaint or fraud report relates to another individual or

business, ONLOK may process information about that individual or entity

for the purposes of investigation, risk assessment, fraud prevention,

dispute resolution, and platform safety, on the basis of ONLOK's legitimate

interests and subject to applicable law.

ONLOK's role in respect of complaints and fraud reports is limited to

investigation, risk assessment, and platform-level action. Where a

complaint or fraud report is substantiated, ONLOK may suspend, restrict,

or revoke the verification status of the vendor or business concerned, and

may publicly indicate, as part of the verification information described in

Section 17, that a verification has been revoked and the general nature

of the reason for the revocation. ONLOK may also escalate substantiated

matters to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, other regulators, or

law-enforcement authorities where appropriate.

ONLOK does not act as a mediator, collection agent, guarantor, or insurer

in respect of any transaction, dispute, or loss between users and vendors.

ONLOK does not guarantee the recovery of funds or goods, or the

resolution of a dispute, and any investigation or resulting action taken by

ONLOK, including suspension or revocation of a vendor's verification

status, does not constitute an admission of liability by ONLOK for any loss

suffered by a user in connection with a vendor's conduct.

ONLOK's intervention in respect of a complaint or fraud report is limited

to matters affecting the accuracy of a vendor's verification status,

suspected fraud, or platform trust and safety. Matters relating to the

general quality of a vendor's goods or services, or the conduct of the

commercial relationship between users (for example, a complaint about

customer service), fall outside ONLOK's verification and complaint-

handling functions and do not, of themselves, form a basis for suspension

or revocation of verification status.

Depending on the nature and severity of a substantiated complaint or

fraud report, ONLOK's response may include one or more of the following:

(a) suspension, restriction, or revocation of the vendor's verification status

and removal from the platform; (b) facilitating the complainant's access

to legal support, including referral to independent legal practitioners who

may assist the complainant in pursuing their own case, at the

complainant's own cost and expense; and/or (c) providing general

guidance on the appropriate steps or channels available to the

complainant for resolving the matter.

For the avoidance of doubt, ONLOK does not fund, underwrite, or bear

responsibility for the cost of any legal action, recovery process, or dispute

resolution pursued by a user or complainant, whether against a vendor or

otherwise, and any such action remains the responsibility of the

complainant.

13. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

ONLOK maintains procedures designed to identify, assess, contain,

investigate, and remediate personal data breaches, and maintains an

internal breach register recording all personal data breaches, including

those falling below the applicable notification threshold.

Where a personal data breach occurs, ONLOK will assess the nature and

risk of the breach and take appropriate measures in accordance with

applicable law. Where a breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and

freedoms of affected individuals, ONLOK will notify the Nigeria Data

Protection Commission without undue delay and, where feasible, within

seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, in accordance

with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms

of affected data subjects, ONLOK will also notify the affected data subjects

without undue delay, together with information on the nature of the

breach and the measures being taken or proposed to address it.

16. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The ONLOK platform is primarily designed for use by adults and

businesses. ONLOK does not knowingly collect, process, verify, or

or other legally recognised basis.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, ONLOK recognises that individuals under

18 may, in appropriate circumstances and where legally permitted, seek

to access ONLOK's services (for example, as young entrepreneurs

conditional on: (i) verified parental or guardian consent, obtained and

recorded in accordance with applicable law; (ii) appropriate age-

verification mechanisms; and (iii) any additional safeguards ONLOK

Where ONLOK becomes aware that personal information belonging to a

  • suspension or restriction of the associated account
  • deletion of the relevant information where legally permissible

  • additional verification or consent requirements

Parents, guardians, or authorised representatives who believe that a

minor may have provided personal information to ONLOK without

appropriate consent may contact the Company through the designated

compliance or privacy communication channels.

ONLOK takes privacy, identity protection, and safety obligations relating

to minors seriously and may implement additional safeguards, verification

controls, or operational restrictions where necessary to maintain

compliance with applicable laws and regulatory obligations.
